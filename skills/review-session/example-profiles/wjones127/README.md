# Example profile: wjones127

Will Jones's review profile after a few days of sessions, mostly on
[Lance](https://github.com/lance-format/lance) and
[LanceDB](https://github.com/lancedb/lancedb).

- `standard.md`: the general standard, plus a "How I review" section drawn
  from his recent review comments.
- `lessons.md`: what the retros learned, with citations to the PRs they came
  from.
- `examples.md`: comments he wrote or approved.
- `comment-style.md`: conventional comments.

Use it as a starting point if you like his taste, or just to see what a
profile looks like after some use. Your own retros will move it toward your
taste.
